Human factors engineering: notes and tools.

Small, careful tools for fitting equipment and workplaces to the people who use them.

Tool Seated Workstation Fit An interactive 3D assessment of a seated workstation. A link-segment manikin is built from individual anthropometric records, its posture is solved from the chair, desk, screens and desk objects, and the result is checked against editable limits: viewing distances and angles, joint angles, clearances and reach zones.
